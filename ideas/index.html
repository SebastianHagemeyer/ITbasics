The Idea Machine

Four slots, one silly sentence, and the smallest version of it you could actually finish. Spin until something makes you laugh, then lock the bits you like and spin the rest.

Switch a slot off if you would rather invent that bit yourself: the sentence leaves the gap and says whose job it is. Game idea deals you a game with a character to steer or a no-character one at random, because plenty of games have nobody to be: in Cookie Clicker you are not the cookie, in Whack-a-Mole you are not the mole, and Tetris and tower defence have no hero either. The line under the tabs says which one you just got. Want them every time? Use the No-character tab.

How to use this properly

      

        

          
1

          
Spin until you laugh

          
The one that makes you snort is the one you will still care about in three weeks. Boring but sensible ideas do not survive week two.

        

        

          
2

          
Lock, re-roll, switch off

          
Love the flamingos but not the maze? Lock the star and spin the rest. Got your own idea for the danger? Switch that slot off and the machine leaves it to you. That is you designing, not the machine.

        

        

          
3

          
Steal one word

          
You are allowed to throw the whole sentence away and keep a single word from it. "Magnetic" on its own has started plenty of good games.

        

        

          
4

          
Build the small version

          
The panel above tells you what version 1 looks like: one thing to steer, one way to win, one way to lose. Build that first, always.

        

      


      
The whole word list

      
Sometimes it's easier to read the lists and mix them yourself. Everything the machine can say is in here.
