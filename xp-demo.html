XP animation sandbox

Every place the site pays XP, mocked up with the reward tuned to what it is worth. The badge, bar and XP number in the header are live and use the real ladder out of xp.js, so awards stack up and a level actually turns over when you cross 445. Coding challenge

50 XP once per challenge

The biggest single payout on the site and the hardest to get, so this is the one that earns real fanfare. Sparks out of the editor, the chip springs in and hangs a beat, the bar visibly jumps a chunk. A student should feel the difference between this and getting a quiz question right.

Module test

25 XP full marks only, best attempt, once per test

Full marks or nothing, which makes the moment sharp: the last question turns green and the whole result panel pays out at once. The default here flies the chip up into the badge, because after a whole test a student should be shown exactly where their XP went. The 8 out of 10 button is worth clicking too: no XP has to feel like no XP, not like a bug.

Freeplay

1 XP first correct answer per scenario

This one fires every few seconds, so it gets the opposite treatment: no screen glow, no sparks, just a small tick that drifts off the answer and a streak counter that bumps. Try the rapid fire button, then switch the feel to Burst and press it again. That second run is the argument for keeping freeplay quiet.

Spot the Error

2 XP first correct answer per scenario, doubled

Same shape as freeplay but worth double, so it needs to look like double without becoming a party. The tick is amber instead of green and the found bug snaps from red to green under the cursor. The colour change does the work: a student learns to read the amber tick as the better one.

Crossing a level

rare

This is not a fifth earner, it is what happens on top of any of the four when the total crosses a rung. Ring wipes out from the middle, confetti, the badge flips to the new number and a banner names the title, but only when the title actually changes: Levels 4 and 5 are both Scripter, so crossing 445 says Level 5 and nothing more. Level 6 at 620 is where Scripter becomes Coder, and that one gets the name.
